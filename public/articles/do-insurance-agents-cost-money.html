Does using a health insurance agent cost more? No — here's what they actually do.

Using a licensed agent costs you nothing. Agents are paid by the insurance carriers, not by you — your premium is exactly the same either way. So the real question isn't "what does it cost." It's "why wouldn't you have one?"

What a good agent does for you, free

  • Finds the right plan, not the obvious one — checks your doctors and prescriptions are covered before you enroll, and that you're getting every subsidy dollar you qualify for.
  • Handles the paperwork — enrollment, life changes (move, marriage, baby, income), and renewals, so your coverage and subsidy don't quietly break.
  • Fights for you when something goes wrong — a denied claim or surprise bill is exactly when you want someone who knows the system in your corner.
  • Stays your person — the same human next year, not a 1-800 maze.

The catch?

There isn't one on price. The only difference between people who have an agent and people who don't is that one group has someone to call.
