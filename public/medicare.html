Medicare, in plain English.

Straight talk: I don't currently offer Medicare plan enrollment, and this page doesn't describe or promote any specific plan. It's education — plus an interest list if you'd like a heads-up when Medicare help opens here.

The four parts, one line each

  • Part A — hospital coverage. Most people pay no premium for it.
  • Part B — doctors and outpatient care. Has a monthly premium.
  • Part C (Medicare Advantage) — private all-in-one alternatives to A+B, often with extras.
  • Part D — prescription drug coverage.

Turning 65 soon? Your initial enrollment window is the 7 months around your 65th birthday — missing it can mean lifelong penalties, so put it on the calendar.
