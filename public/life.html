Life insurance in 60 seconds: which kind actually fits you?

Three questions. No rates, no pressure — just the right starting point, in plain English.

1. What's the main job you need done?

2. Your age range?

3. Monthly amount you'd comfortably set aside?

Your starting point: Term life. Big protection for the years your family depends on your income, at the lowest cost per dollar of coverage. Simple, and usually the right first policy.
Your starting point: Whole life. Coverage that never expires and builds cash value you can use — costs more than term, but it's permanent. Often paired with a term policy.
Your starting point: An annuity. You set money aside now (or roll over savings) in exchange for guaranteed income later. Suitability rules apply — a licensed review is required, and that's a good thing.
